Privacy Policy for Golden Brick CRM

Application ID: com.reos.goldenbrick
Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Golden Brick Ventures ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Golden Brick CRM mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle user data in compliance with enterprise standards and Google Play policies.

1. Information We Collect

Golden Brick CRM is an enterprise customer relationship management and real estate booking application. We collect:

  • Account Details: Name, enterprise email address, phone number, and credentials provisioned by your organization.
  • CRM & Booking Data: Lead contact information, client communications, property unit booking details, and payment milestone records.
  • Uploaded Documents: Documents, identity verification proofs, and photos uploaded directly by users for property transaction records.
  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, and crash logs to monitor app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Data

Collected data is used strictly for legitimate business operations:

  • Managing client leads, scheduling site visits, and recording unit bookings.
  • Tracking payment milestones and generating transaction receipts.
  • Providing secure role-based access to enterprise CRM data.

3. Advertising and Tracking

No Advertising: Golden Brick CRM contains NO commercial advertisements. We do not use Google Advertising ID (AAID), ad networks, or ad-targeting SDKs.

4. Data Sharing and Security

  • We do not sell or trade user data to third parties.
  • All network communications between the App and our backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Enterprise data is stored in secured cloud infrastructure with role-based access control.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

User accounts are managed by your organization's administrative team. Users may request deletion of their personal data or account records by contacting their organization admin or emailing admin@goldenbrickventures.com.

6. Children's Privacy

This enterprise application is designed exclusively for business professionals aged 18 and older. We do not collect information from children.
